Branch Visit Scorecard, Redesigned

A concept for turning the Branch Audit sheet from a place scores get typed into a tracker that shows which branches got visited, what we found, who owns the fix, and whether it was fixed by the next visit.

Draft 1 · 10/22/2026Source: Branch Audit sheet, SCTX + DFWT + SAN tabs, read 10/22Brian Herzig · proposal for Q4 build

01What the current sheet tells us

I read every scored visit on the SCTX tab, 22 visits from 5/23/2025 to 9/25/2026. The sheet does its main job (it records a score) but it can't answer the two questions the rock is about: are we visiting enough, and is the branch getting better because we visited.

4.65 / 5

Every visit since Q3 2025 scored 4.6 or higher. The average across all 22 is 4.65. When almost everything is a 5, the score can't separate a strong branch from an average one.

12 of 22

"All trucks left on time" scored a 3 on 12 of 22 visits, at every branch. It's the most repeated gap on the sheet, and nothing on the sheet shows it was ever assigned or checked again.

14 months

Houston's last scored visit was 8/22/2025. A 1/23 row was started and left blank. Nothing on the sheet flags it, so a gap like that goes unnoticed.

0

No follow-up tracking anywhere. Notes exist on 6 of 22 visits. No owner, due date, or re-check column. The "success" part of a visit is invisible.

3 tabs

Three different rubrics. DFWT has no date column and a different dispatch standard. SAN has no fleet or yard rows. The 9/23 and 9/25 visits sit under a second "Q2 - 2026" header (they're Q3).

1 score

One score per branch, not per manager. Sales blocks, EOD, and huddles are owned by specific people. A branch average hides who to coach. The SAN tab tried per-manager rows once (5/27/25) and stopped.

03How the sheet is built

Same Google Sheet, new tabs. The old SCTX, DFWT and SAN tabs move to an Archive tab untouched, so the history stays. Every new tab reads from one entry point, so a visit is typed in once.

Tab 1

Dashboard

  • Everything in section 02, all formulas, nothing typed by hand
  • Region and quarter dropdowns at the top, so Mike can use the same sheet for DFWT
Tab 2

Visit Log

  • One row per visit: date, branch, region, quarter (auto), visitor, managers present
  • Three checkboxes: scorecard, huddle audit, circle-up. All three checked = full audit, which is what counts toward the rock
  • Overall score, top win, top fix
Tab 3

Item Scores

  • One row per item per visit (long format), so trends and per-item history are one formula
  • Owner role on every row (BM / Ops / FM / branch), so scores roll up to a manager, not only to the branch
  • "Not observed" is its own value and is left out of averages, unlike today's mix of NA, dash and blank
Tab 4

Action Items

  • Any item scored below standard creates a row: owner, due date, Monday pulse link, status
  • Re-check column fills at the next visit to that branch: improved, same, worse
  • This is the tab that answers "did the visit work"
Tab 5

Rubric

  • One rubric for both regions, with the scoring definitions that now live in row 2 of each tab
  • Version number at the top. Changing a standard means a new version, not an edit in place
Entry

Phone form

  • A Google Form you fill on your phone as the day goes, in the order the day happens
  • Feeds the Visit Log and Item Scores tabs. No typing into a wide sheet on site

04The rubric, reorganized around the day

Same 20 items you already score, grouped into five sections that follow the visit from arrival to EOD. Two changes do most of the work.

Change 1: meeting the standard is a 3, not a 5

5

Better than the standard. You'd show it to another branch as the example.

3

Meets the standard as written. This is a good day.

1

Below standard. Creates an action item automatically.

NO

Not observed today. Left out of the average, never scored as a zero.

Today a branch that simply does its job scores 5 across the board, so the sheet has nowhere to go. With 3 as the standard, a 4.6 means something again, and Super Green can be set at a real bar (for example, a 3.5 average with no 1s).

05How a visit runs with this

Week before

Schedule

Visit goes on the Visit Log with a date. The coverage grid shows it dashed. Pull the branch's open action items from last time.

On site

Score as you go

Phone form, section by section through the day. Each score below standard asks for a one-line note.

Circle-up

Agree the fixes

Walk the managers through the 1s and the repeat 3s. Each one gets an owner and a date before you leave.

Same day

Pulse it

Action items go to the branch Main Board, pulse link pasted back into the Action Items tab.

Next visit

Re-check

Score last visit's flagged items first. Improved or not, it lands in the Re-check column.

06What "success" means on this sheet

Coverage

Did we show up

  • Full audits per quarter: 3 of 5 Green, 5 of 5 Super Green
  • No branch goes two quarters without one
Follow-through

Did the fixes happen

  • Share of action items closed by their due date
  • Proposed target: 80%
Improvement

Did the visit change anything

  • Share of flagged items that scored higher on re-check
  • Repeat findings, like trucks out late, show up by name

The overall score stays, but it moves to a supporting role. A branch that scores 4.9 and fixes nothing should look worse on this sheet than one that scores 3.4 and closes every item.

07Calls for you before I build it

  1. Re-scale so 3 = meets standard?The biggest change, and it resets every branch's number. Old visits stay on the archive tab on the old scale. They don't get converted.
  2. Score per manager, or per branch with an owner on each item?Per-item owner is lighter and is what's drawn here. Full per-manager scoring is more accurate but roughly triples the entry on a 3-manager branch.
  3. One sheet for both regions?The dashboard is built to filter by region. Needs Mike to agree to one rubric. The dispatch standard is the only real difference today.
  4. Use it at North Austin tomorrow?The form won't be ready by 10/23. My suggestion: score on the current SCTX tab as usual, but write down an owner and date for every fix. Those become the first rows on the Action Items tab.
